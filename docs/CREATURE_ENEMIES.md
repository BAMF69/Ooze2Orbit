# Creature enemies

Review draft for BAMF69. All new names, numbers, templates and encounter rules below are proposals. Timings are seconds in tell / active strike / recovery order. HP is per enemy and damage is per committed attack. Water's four regular enemies use the current `ENEMIES` values. E/M/H mean Easy/Medium/Hard.

Current build: Root Lane and the three authored Jungle Medium rooms are playable. Their patrol starts are seeded. Jungle Hard, Vine sweeper and procedural Jungle placement are pending.

**Playtest baseline.** Seed 7, real Creature scene from the stage menu, with stepped inputs. I walked the shoreline and explored the Easy entry, then verified Medium's gills lock and supplied gills for a focused Medium test. A direct approach to the Medium net cost 2 of 6 HP. I armed it and trapped a healthy enemy without biting. Easy's full clear and a natural generation-to-Medium transition remain unverified.

## Encounter kit and lineage

1. Perceive from an authored anchor or medium-specific patrol. Use the room's actual collision/visibility and cover rules. Sample player position, height, speed and medium as needed. Patrol anchors keep swimmers in water, droppers beneath their perch and rollers on their slope.
2. Request a tell when the target is eligible and the room scheduler can reserve a safe answer. Freeze target direction, footprint and destination. Facing, pose and a persistent lane marker communicate attack shape, with audio optional. Offscreen cues identify direction and the dangerous boundary.
3. Commit the fixed dash, leap, drop or pulse. A blocked path ends in recovery. Damage is active only inside the announced footprint, once per commitment. Overlapping threat checks account for currents, stun, vent cycles and movement from the minimum supported build.
4. Recover on a defined landing or stop. Use a common recovery vulnerability and trap capture contract. Return to patrol, reusing existing room-local net, guarded control and subdual behavior where it fits.

Use engine-free decisions with explicit inputs for time, room geometry queries, encounter state and seeded variation. Room substreams derive from world seed, generator version and stable room/slot IDs. Keep timings, reach, speeds, climate modifiers and detection in one data source. Behind the creature debug flag show phase and timer, perception and occlusion, intended path and attack footprint, safe lanes and why a tell was denied.

Credit an aggressive recovery bite or fight resolution, a cautious covered pass, and an adaptable trap or meaningful lane/trait switch once per authored obstacle per life. Attribute food only when eaten. Allow a different resolution after a failed attempt and cap repeated actions before the daughter roll. Record the action and trait together, such as “You waited below the Needle swimmer with gills, then crossed during its recovery.” Bias the relevant variation and let the player choose a daughter. Preserve the existing diet, normalized temperament shares, cause log and stage shift cap. Keep food preference separate from temperament and room permissions. Herbivores can fight, carnivores can sneak and omnivores can commit to either route. Show at least one contrasting daughter with a useful alternative at review.

## Generator contract

- Place the graph, required rewards, controls, return paths and recovery first. Populate tagged authored anchors with the compatible roster above. Water names in the tables are actual roles handled by `buildRoom`. New names identify proposed templates and their room roles. In new biomes reuse the traversal, guarded control, trap encounter, sealed arena and goal contracts with biome-specific collision and anchor sets.
- Keep the nursery, vault, recovery, underchannel, shoreline and arrivals free of attack pressure. A loop remains available under its own route rules without adding a chase. Keep every entrance clear of pits and enemies within 60 px, with at least 1.2 seconds from arrival to any possible hit. Check every reciprocal direction and vertical arrival.
- A sealed fight requires fight or nonlethal clearance. Verify a healthy-enemy trap-only solution, including every rearm, with baseline permitted traits. Seal forward progress and preserve a retreat or reset. Sneak-through answers in the tables require an open traversal or guardian bypass. Guarded controls need a real line-of-sight break and a two-second safe operating window. A dropper always has a lateral dodge lane. Reserve control, latch, claim, rig and spawn space outside any unavoidable strike.
- Check minimum traits including standalone lineage, keys before gates, banked samples before inheritance, all class variants and Gardener non-circularity. Optional branches and stat gates remain outside critical progress. Water Medium assumes gills, Hard assumes gills, fins and diversion. Easy assumes baseline movement. A pressure crossing keeps expiry retreat, emergency release and 25% minimum-build slack. A sprint-only bot fails rooms with a required deliberate solution while intentional bypasses remain valid.
- Cap Hard concurrency at one damaging window per shared refuge unless controller replays prove a joint escape. Keep a recovery cache within three rooms of a Hard spike, at most two consecutive sealed rooms and at most three consecutive critical rooms of one role. Validate relevant room-state and climate combinations against the controller, then reject incompatible placements with a bounded retry and authored fallback. Version intentional fingerprint changes and preserve legacy save replay.

## Risks and review questions

- Timing is a starting point. Six baseline HP makes a 3-damage hit consequential. Confirm the player can read the painted silhouettes, water overlays and floor markers in portrait and reduced motion, then adjust timing or geometry before raising damage.
- Check whether a dry or sheltered route feels like a distinct play style and yields enough food to support the return trip. Compare fight, stealth and environment resolutions at the same lineage strength. A direct fight needs a worthwhile opening, and cautious passage needs an observable decision.
- Decide whether guardian bypass clears the guardian requirement for a Hard seal. This draft grants the seal for any authored guardian solution with the normal claim and bank steps. Decide whether to retain temporary Water rain/drought alongside the four generational levers. This draft retains both, with permanent Gardener sluice effects independent.
- Confirm whether Water's Weirback should replace the existing finale trio or precede it after a recovery pocket. This draft tests it as a separate approach first and keeps total route pressure in view.
- Scope thin ice, foliage, slip and warmth as authored geometry or explicit interactions first. Defer richer predator ecology and adaptive boss variants until the baseline counters are fun.

4. Build the Survey Gallery once two biome routes work, then verify all seal pairs as the remaining routes arrive. Check lineage causes and civilization handoff.
