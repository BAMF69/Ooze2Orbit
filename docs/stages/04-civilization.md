- **Pitch:** your creatures notice you. Every intervention is now an omen.
- **Lens:** continent, then planet.
- **God mode:** fewer, costlier, riskier tools. A flood reads as punishment, a good harvest as a blessing. How the
  species treats the Gardener (worship, fear, resentment) follows the gentle vs. harsh tracker.
- **Avatar mode:** a leader or prophet; what they do becomes the civilization's myths.
- **Culture from environment:** island species become sailors, harsh climates make cooperative societies, diet shapes
  the economy (farmers, hunters, traders).
- **`T0|▼|` hint:** myths of two gods, the Watcher (the Gardener) and the Maker. A `T0|▼|` probe crashes.
  **Choice:** adopting its tech speeds the species up but makes them easier for `T0|▼|` to read in the space stage.
