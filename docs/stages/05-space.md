# Stage 5: Space (outline)

> **Outline only, not started.** Nothing here gets coded until the stages before it are fun. When this becomes the
> active stage, rebuild it from `docs/stages/_TEMPLATE.md`. Written 2026-11-08 from `docs/LORE.md`; items marked
> *(approved)* were decided by BAMF69, the rest are *(Claude's pick)*.

- **Pitch:** three species from three worlds meet, build one fleet, and face `T0|▼|`.
- **Reference game:** Endless Sky (top-down 2D ships, a star map, trade, missions, outfitting a fleet). Mechanics only.
- **Win (the game's win):** defeat `T0|▼|` with a fleet built from three spacefaring species *(approved)*.
- **Ship tech from temperament** *(approved)*: cautious species build strong shields, aggressive ones strong weapons,
  adaptable ones fast, mobile engines. Diet adds a second layer (plant eaters: industry and repair; meat eaters:
  salvage and raiding).
- **`T0|▼|` adapts** *(approved)*: after every battle it adapts to the ship tech it faced, so the player must keep
  creating or discovering new ship technology. Sources: ongoing evolution, combining parts from different species,
  exploring ruins, salvaging `T0|▼|` wrecks (strong, but it already knows how to counter them).
- **Species replacement** *(approved)*: a spacefaring species can die out; a new one is generated to take its place.
- **Modes** *(approved)*: solo (the player raised all three species); connected (other players' or AI-generated species
  fill the universe; needs online play, out of scope for now).
- **Played on its own:** starts from three default or generated species.
- **Receives:** three spacefaring species, per `docs/HANDOFF_CONTRACT.md`.
